About the Event

Lyra Fest is a music festival where people come together to enjoy live music and share a special experience.

The event takes place on Saturday, March 21, 2026, at Riverside Park in Buenos Aires.

The festival begins at 2:00 PM and ends at 11:00 PM. It is a full day dedicated to music and entertainment.

What to Expect

-Live Music

Enjoy different live performances throughout the day and discover new music.

-Festival Atmosphere

Meet other music lovers, spend time with friends and enjoy the festival atmosphere.

Activities

During the festival, visitors can enjoy the following activities:

  • Live music
  • Food and drinks
  • Art activities
  • Outdoor activities
